# Code of conduct

Seamless Migrate is built by people and, with their supervision, by AI agents, for operators who
run it against production workloads. We want a project where anyone can report a failed migration,
question a design, review a change or ask for help without fear.

## Expectations

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with the work, not the person.
- Give feedback that is specific, evidence-based and actionable; accept it in the same spirit.
- Welcome newcomers and people who are not native English speakers. Issues may be written in
  English or Bahasa Indonesia.
- Keep other people's data out of the project: no customer names, hostnames, credentials or logs
  from real environments in issues, pull requests or chat.
- Credit the work of others, upstream os-migrate included.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, doxxing, sustained
disruption of discussions, and publishing information shared in confidence.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break these
expectations, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban contributors who do so. Decisions
are explained to the person concerned.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainer named in [CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md), or, if the
concern involves the maintainer, through GitHub's private reporting for this repository. Reports are
handled confidentially.
